# Book Launch Timeline

A simple, free tool for authors. Pick your book launch date, and get a backward checklist you can actually use — saved right in your browser.

Built by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**.

## What It Does

This app helps you plan your book launch without any stress. You pick your launch date, and it gives you a ready-made checklist working backward from launch day:

| Task | Timing | What you'll do |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Send ARC copies to readers | 6 weeks before launch | Distribute complimentary review copies to beta readers so you gather early reviews |
| Set up preorder on KDP | 4 weeks before launch | Enable preorder on your publishing platform so readers can reserve their copy |
| Launch ad campaign | 2 weeks before launch | Start your targeted ad campaign to build momentum toward launch day |
| Send newsletter announcement | 1 week before launch | Email your subscriber list about the upcoming launch and preorder |
| Final pre-launch checklist | 1 day before launch | Verify preorder is live, ads are running, and everything is ready |

Mark each task complete as you go — your progress saves automatically in your browser.

## How to Use It

1. **Open `index.html`** in any web browser. No install, no sign-up, no server.
2. **Pick your launch date** using the date picker.
3. **Work through your checklist** earliest-first. Mark each item complete when you're done.
4. **Come back later** — your saved state is waiting in your browser's localStorage.
5. **Clear your data** anytime using the "Clear all data" button at the bottom.

That's it. Nothing leaves your device. No account, no tracking, no API keys.
